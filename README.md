## ⚡ About Me

I design and build **intelligent, high-throughput, low-latency systems** that operate under strict real-world constraints. Currently completing my **B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science** at **NMIMS University, Navi Mumbai** (STME), my focus is on engineering production-ready ML inference gateways, autonomous agentic loops, and decoupled streaming architectures—not just prototype notebooks.

* 🔭 **Active Engineering Focus:** Autonomous payment workflows via Model Context Protocol (MCP), sub-2ms payment risk engines, Sybil graph sentinels, and event-driven stock market telemetry.
* 🧠 **Core Competencies:** Real-Time ML Inference (<2ms SLAs) · Autonomous Agents (MCP & Tool Calling) · Event-Driven Microservices · Time Series & Quantitative Systems · Graph Anomaly Detection · Computer Vision.
* 🏫 **Research Background:** Former Computer Vision Research Intern at **VJTI Mumbai** (Engineered YOLOv8 & OpenCV pipelines for High Security Registration Plate / ANPR recognition).
* ⚡ **Leadership & Community:** Former Co-Head of **Infinix AI&ML Club**, NMIMS (organized hands-on workshops and industry tech talks featuring speakers from IBM, BrowserStack, Xenvolt.AI) | Ex-Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador.
* 🎯 **Engineering Philosophy:** *"If it can't handle live network latency, high-concurrency throughput, and messy edge cases in production, it's just a demo."*
* ☕ **Fun Fact:** I mass-produce commits before breakfast.

## 🌟 Flagship Deployments

### 🛡️ [RazorShield AI — Real-Time Risk Engine & Autonomous Dispute Representment](https://github.com/Suryanshsaraf/razorpaytrack02)
> *Built for Razorpay AI Buildathon 2026 — Track 02 (AI Risk Manager)*  
> [![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-Console-00D924?style=flat-square&logo=google-chrome&logoColor=white)](https://suryanshsaraf.github.io/razorpaytrack02/)
> [![Pitch Video](https://img.shields.io/badge/Pitch_Video-3:54_Demo-FF0000?style=flat-square&logo=youtube&logoColor=white)](https://youtu.be/GDoga27Ewnk)
> [![Docker](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docker-Ready-2496ED?style=flat-square&logo=docker&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Suryanshsaraf/razorpaytrack02/blob/main/docker-compose.yml)
> [![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3.10+-3776AB?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white)](https://www.python.org/)

A high-throughput, multi-modal risk mitigation system engineered for synchronous payment rails:
* ⚡ **Sub-2ms Decision Latency:** Quantized LightGBM + true binary Protobuf ONNX Runtime inference ($1.40\text{ms}$ single-sample) satisfying strict gateway checkout SLAs.
* 🛡️ **Visa Compelling Evidence 3.0 (CE 3.0) Agent:** Automated dispute dossier compiler correlating 3DS tokens, prior settled orders, and carrier GPS delivery telemetry (96% dispute win probability).
* 🕸️ **NetworkX Sybil Ring Sentinel:** Heterogeneous entity bipartite graph isolating coordinated card testing botnets and mutated Indian COD address rings.
* 📊 **Cost-Utility Objective Function:** Calibrated threshold tuning slashing False Positive Rate from $2.04\% \to 0.21\%$ and saving ₹15.5M+ in held-out merchant GMV.

> *Enterprise-grade, event-driven microservices platform for high-frequency market data*  
> [![Kafka](https://img.shields.io/badge/Streaming-Apache_Kafka-231F20?style=flat-square&logo=apachekafka&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Suryanshsaraf/market-telemetry-ai)
> [![FastAPI](https://img.shields.io/badge/Backend-FastAPI_SSE-009688?style=flat-square&logo=fastapi&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Suryanshsaraf/market-telemetry-ai)
